PO Flag Bot

Daily · 6:00 PM

Makes sure every product owner logs at least one new idea for each client, every working day.

  1. At 6:00 PM, checks each PO's clients for an idea logged today on the client's Notion content board.
  2. Anyone who missed a client is flagged in their Slack channel, with the missing clients and their count.
  3. The flag is recorded in a Notion tracker for that weekday (1 to 5).
  4. At the end of the week, a summary of everyone flagged is sent to me as a DM.

Ideation Tracker → Content Board

On every entry

Strategy ideas used to be copied onto each client's board by hand. Now they land there on their own.

  1. The strategy team logs ideas in the Notion Ideation Tracker with a final edit link and a ⭐.
  2. Zapier picks up the starred entry and finds that client's content board.
  3. Every starred idea is added to the board's Ideas category, ready for the PO.
  4. The new cards feed the Strategy Execution Dashboard, which starts each idea's 7-day clock.

Client onboarding → Slack

On new client

Every new client gets their own Slack channel, with leadership already in it, the moment onboarding is confirmed.

  1. A bot posts the onboarding message in a Slack channel. That message is the trigger.
  2. Zapier creates the client's Slack channel.
  3. Top management are added to the channel automatically.
  4. I get a DM to start the client's Notion setup (next flow).

Notion ↔ Slack canvas sync

Always on

A live canvas in each client channel that mirrors the client's record in the Notion Project Tracker.

  1. From the DM, I duplicate the Notion board and add the client to the Project Tracker.
  2. I create a canvas from my template in the client's channel and add the sync bot.
  3. I paste the channel ID and canvas ID into the client's Project Tracker row.
  4. From then on, every Project Tracker update appears in the Slack canvas automatically.
