PO Pace Dashboard

Flags content cards across every client board before a review deadline is missed.

33+client boards in one view
3urgency levels
0boards opened by hand

The problem

Content status was spread across more than 30 separate Notion boards. Late cards were usually found after the post date had already passed.

How it works

  • Reads the Project Tracker to find every active client and their content board.
  • Checks each card against its publish date: Critical if review was missed, High if review is due today, Heads-up if it's behind pace.
  • Reads each client's review lead time from the team's cadence sheet, with a 24-hour default.
  • One click copies the flag list for Slack, per PO or for everyone.
